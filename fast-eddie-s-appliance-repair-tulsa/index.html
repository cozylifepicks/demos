What gets repaired

Kitchen and laundry, sorted

Most appliance problems are one worn part away from working again. The job is finding which one — and being honest when a machine isn't worth the repair.

Refrigerator Repair

Not cooling, icing over, running constantly. One Google review describes an LG compressor replaced after other companies "wouldn't even bother to check it out".

Dishwasher Repair & Install

Won't drain, won't fill, won't start. Old unit pulled out and the new one fitted when a replacement makes more sense — a customer's review describes exactly that.

Washer Repair

Leaks, drum noise, cycles that stop halfway, water that never drains away. Diagnosed and repaired where the machine stands.

Dryer Repair

No heat, long drying times, drums that squeal or won't turn. Usually a heating element, belt or thermostat — parts that are worth replacing.

Oven & Range Repair

Elements that won't heat, temperatures that drift, doors that won't seal, burners that won't light. Gas and electric.

Warranty Parts Handled

When a part is still under manufacturer warranty, it gets ordered and fitted for you. "They helped with, ordered the warranty parts" — a real Google review.
